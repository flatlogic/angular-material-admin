ApexCharts is offered under a **dual-license model** to support individuals, startups, and commercial products of all sizes.

- Use by companies or entities at or above $2M in annual revenue, budget or funding

Pro and Premium are priced per developer, and priced on the features you use rather than on your size.

🚫 Not allowed:

- Redistribution in toolkits, SDKs, or platforms

- Competing charting products

- **Free, at Community and above:** every chart type and feature not listed below.
